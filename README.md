# Cinder Reef — A Dante Cruz Story

[![Competition](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)
[![Rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/abilyusa/gta6-01-entry-v6/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)
[![Reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/abilyusa/gta6-01-entry-v6/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)

An open-world crime game on a fictional volcanic island. Dante Cruz takes
on eight story missions across seven districts — steal cars, race the
streets, crack the bank safe and the casino vault, and escape the whole
Cinder Reef police force, helicopter included. Mara "Ghost" Reyes joins
the crew after mission 3 and can blind the chopper on your signal.

- **8 sequential story missions**, $21,400 total payout, from a $800
  car theft at the docks to a $10,000 casino vault crack.
- **Escalating police dispatch**: patrol cars, roadblocks at 3 stars,
  RAM/PIT at 4 stars, the CONDOR-1 helicopter at 5 stars.
- **Crew ability (Q)**: Mara disables the helicopter for 20s and drops
  your wanted level by 1 (45s cooldown).
- **Garage with 3 slots** in the Docks — delivered cars stay parked
  there, ready to drive again.
- **Live minimap, full HUD** (cash, wanted stars, objective, speed,
  health, ammo), traffic, pedestrians, exploding cars, synthesized
  WebAudio sound (engine, sirens, shots, pickups).

## Controls

WASD / arrows — drive & walk · E — enter/exit car, carjack, crack safes
· Mouse aim + click — shoot · Q — crew ability · R — restart mission
· ESC — pause

## Play

Serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html`, or run
`node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080. No build step, no
network, no assets — everything is drawn and synthesized in code.
